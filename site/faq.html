About Agent Alla

What is Agent Alla?

Agent Alla ("Alla") is an AI assistant, built by ROILITY, S.L., that discusses real estate investment, taxes, zoning, and financing scenarios in Spain. You describe a property, plot, or deal, and Alla works through the full cost of entry, a realistic exit value, and the risks that could move either number — comparing strategies (flip, buy-to-let, staged hybrid) side by side instead of handing you a single ready-made recommendation.

Is Alla a lawyer, notary, gestor, tax advisor, or bank?

No. Alla's replies are generated by an AI model and are meant as a starting point for your own research, not a final legal, tax, financial, or investment decision. Always confirm anything that affects money, contracts, or legal standing with a qualified professional before acting on it — see our Terms of Service.

What can I ask Alla about?

Typical questions cover a specific property, plot, or deal: purchase and renovation cost, realistic exit value, rental yield and ROI, taxes (ITP, IVA, plusvalía, non-resident income tax), zoning and planning status, and comparing ownership strategies such as flip vs. buy-to-let vs. a staged hybrid.

Which AI model powers Alla, and is my data used to train it?

Alla's replies are generated using Anthropic's Claude models. Your conversations are not used to train Anthropic's models, and information from one client's or agency's conversations is never carried into another's. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Yes. Web subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from your account page — access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. App Store subscriptions are managed and cancelled through your Apple ID Account Settings.

Privacy & security

How is my data protected?

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to account and conversation data is limited by role. ROILITY, S.L. is the data controller under the GDPR and Spain's LOPDGDD — see our Privacy Policy for what's collected, why, and your rights (access, correction, deletion, and more).

Who sees my conversations with Alla?

Only the minimum needed to run the Service: your message is sent to Anthropic to generate a reply, and billing data is handled by Stripe or Apple. We don't sell your data or share it with agencies or advertisers outside what's needed to run the Service you signed up for.
